Paris — the city of light, love, and endless layers. Every season spins a different dream over its timeless boulevards.

# Spring: A Love Letter in Bloom (March - May)
Paris in spring is a painter's reverie. Chestnut trees awaken, blossoms lace the air around the Seine, and cafe terraces hum with lazy conversations. Strolls through Jardin des Tuileries and Parc des Buttes-Chaumont feel like drifting inside an Impressionist canvas.

It's also festival season — Fête de la Musique and open-air markets bring vibrancy to every arrondissement. Spring is the time for romance — soft, dreamy, a little rainy, infinitely beautiful.

# Summer: Sun-drenched Splendor (June - August)
As summer matures, Paris explodes in energy. Long sunny days, riverside pop-up beaches (Paris Plages), and endless rooftop soirées. Museums and monuments buzz with visitors, yet there's also a holiday lull — many Parisians leave for the coast, gifting the city a quieter heartbeat.

Picnic by the Canal Saint-Martin, lose yourself in evening strolls along the Seine. It's warm, it's lively — if you can bear the crowds, Paris in summer is a golden dream.

# Autumn: The Golden Metropolis (September - November)
Autumn is Paris at its most thoughtful. Leaves rustle down cobbled streets, wine festivals kick up their heels, and art season bursts into life. This is when Parisians return home and the city feels like itself again — confident, chic, poetic.

The light sharpens; the cafés glow warmly against the early dusk. It's the perfect season for museum-hopping and reading Sartre over an espresso.

# Winter: A Parisian Dreamscape (December - February)
Paris in winter is elegantly bare, its monuments standing crisp and clear against pale skies. Christmas markets sparkle, the Champs-Élysées wears her finest lights, and patisseries serve up steaming chocolat chaud and delicate galette des rois.

Winter is quieter, more intimate. Ideal for those who seek the soul of Paris beyond its postcards.
